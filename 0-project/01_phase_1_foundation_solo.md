# Phase 1 — Foundation: Solo Operator

## MAP

```text
START
  │
  ▼
Define niche + service area
  │
  ├── Rexburg → students / landlords / move-outs
  └── Idaho Falls → families / recurring homes
  │
  ▼
Launch 4 core offers
  │
  ├── Recurring Home Cleaning
  ├── Student Move-Out Cleaning
  ├── Landlord Turnover Cleaning
  └── Premium Deep / Home Reset
  │
  ▼
Create simple fixed pricing
  │
  ├── Base price by beds/baths
  └── Clearly priced add-ons
  │
  ▼
Build minimum online system
  │
  ├── Website
  ├── Instant quote form
  ├── Online booking request
  ├── Stripe/payment
  └── Customer checklist
  │
  ▼
Acquire first customers
  │
  ├── Facebook/local groups
  ├── Google Business Profile
  ├── Student groups
  ├── Flyers / QR codes
  ├── Landlord outreach

  ▼

  ├── Before photos

  │
  ├── Revenue/job

  └── Repeatable service process
  │
```

## Primary goal

Prove that customers will repeatedly pay for a reliable, transparent cleaning service before hiring employees.

## Best initial positioning

> **Reliable home cleaning with upfront pricing and no guesswork.**

For Rexburg, emphasize:

> **Student move-out and landlord turnover cleaning.**

For Idaho Falls, emphasize:

> **Reliable recurring home cleaning.**

## Initial pricing framework

Use pricing as a starting hypothesis rather than a permanent price sheet.

| Offer | Target range |
|---|---:|
| Recurring standard clean | $120–$200 |
| Deep clean | $175–$400+ |
| Move-out | $180–$400+ |
| Premium home reset | $250–$500+ |
| Oven add-on | $20–$35 |
| Refrigerator add-on | $20–$35 |
| Interior cabinets | $25–$50 |
| Hard-water treatment | $50–$125+ |

## Phase 1 differentiators

- Exact or highly predictable pricing
- Clearly defined inclusions
- 30-minute arrival window
- Before/after photos
- Digital checklist
- Easy rescheduling
- Same cleaner whenever possible
- Professional communication
- Automatic review request
- Recurring booking option

## Do NOT build yet

- Full mobile app
- Large employee team
- Complex route optimization
- Commercial cleaning
- Dozens of service types
- Expensive branding
- Large equipment inventory

## Exit criteria

Move to Phase 2 when:

- You have consistent weekly demand.
- Recurring customers provide a predictable revenue base.
- You know your average job time and profit.
- Your checklist produces consistent quality.
- Customers regularly leave positive reviews.
- You are turning away work because of capacity or scheduling.
