## Primary goal

Stop thinking of the company as a cleaning crew and start operating it as a **repeatable home-services platform**.

## Core product

The customer experience should become:

```text
ENTER ADDRESS
      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓

      ↓
CUSTOMER APPROVES
      ↓
AUTOMATIC REBOOKING
```

## Subscription model

### Weekly
For customers who want the home continuously maintained.

### Biweekly
Likely the primary recurring residential plan.

### Monthly
Lower-frequency maintenance.

### Home Reset
Premium recurring package combining cleaning with rotating add-ons.

Example:

> Every month: standard cleaning  
> Every quarter: oven/fridge/hard-water add-on  
> Every six months: deeper reset

The objective is to make cleaning feel like a household utility rather than a one-time purchase.

## Trust architecture

Make trust visible throughout the product:

- Background-checked cleaners
- Insured service
- Cleaner profile
- Arrival notification
- Defined service checklist
- Before/after documentation
- Quality guarantee
- Easy issue reporting
- Consistent cleaner assignment

### Airbnb Reset

### Home Reset
Cleaning + organization + rotating deep-clean tasks.

### Realtor Ready
Listing preparation / pre-sale cleaning.

## Expansion strategy

Do NOT immediately expand geographically.

First achieve:

```text
Rexburg
  ↓
Repeatable operations
  ↓
Idaho Falls
  ↓
Combined market dominance
  ↓
Prove unit economics
  ↓
New city
```

The ideal new market has:

- Dense housing
- Strong rental market
- College population or transient renters
- Property managers
- Household income capable of paying for cleaning
- Manageable driving distances
- Limited highly professional competitors

## Long-term competitive advantage

The cleaning itself is relatively easy to copy.

The moat should become:

### 1. Customer database
Recurring customers and historical preferences.

### 2. Operational data
Actual cleaning times and profitability by home type.

### 3. Brand
Known for showing up and doing exactly what was promised.

### 4. Technology
Frictionless booking and communication.

### 5. B2B relationships
Landlords, property managers and realtors.

### 6. Standardization
A new cleaner should be able to reproduce the same experience.

## Ultimate positioning

> **The easiest and most reliable way to keep your home clean.**

Not:

> "We are a local cleaning company."

The end-state brand should sell **convenience, reliability and peace of mind**, with cleaning as the underlying service.
