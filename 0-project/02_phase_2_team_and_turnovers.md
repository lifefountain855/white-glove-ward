## Primary goal

Turn a solo cleaning service into a repeatable local operation that can handle more customers without requiring the owner to clean every home.

## Hiring sequence

### Hire #1
A dependable cleaner who can reproduce your checklist.

### Hire #2
A second cleaner to increase capacity and allow two-person jobs.

### Hire #3+
Build small teams around geographic/service clusters.

## Operational system

Every job should have:

1. Customer information
2. Service type
3. Room checklist
4. Special instructions
5. Estimated duration
6. Cleaner assignment
7. Before-job status
8. After-job checklist
9. Photos where appropriate
10. Customer rating
11. Payment status

## B2B strategy

### Highest-priority targets

1. Student housing landlords
2. Property managers
3. Apartment complexes
4. Realtors
5. Airbnb/short-term rental operators

### Offer

> **Turnover cleaning that is ready for the next tenant, with completion photos and a standardized checklist.**

This is particularly powerful in Rexburg because student housing creates predictable turnover periods.

## Seasonal strategy

### April / May
Push move-out cleaning.

### June / July
Push deep cleans, recurring family cleaning and landlord preparation.

### August
Push student move-in/move-out and turnover cleaning.

### September–March
Focus on recurring residential customers and B2B accounts.

## Phase 2 technology

Upgrade the website into a lightweight operations platform:

- Customer accounts
- Online booking
- Automated confirmations
- Recurring billing
- Cleaner schedules
- Digital checklists
- Photo uploads
- Review automation
- Customer history

## Key metrics

| Metric | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Revenue per labor hour | Core profitability |
| Jobs per cleaner/day | Capacity |
| Customer retention | Recurring revenue |
| Re-clean rate | Quality |
| Average review score | Reputation |
| Customer acquisition cost | Marketing |
| Recurring revenue % | Stability |
| B2B revenue % | Scale |

## Exit criteria

Move to Phase 3 when:

- Multiple cleaners can perform the service consistently.
- You have repeatable hiring/training.
- Scheduling is becoming a meaningful administrative burden.
- B2B accounts generate recurring jobs.
- Owner time is increasingly spent on sales/management rather than cleaning.
