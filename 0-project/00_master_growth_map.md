# The strategic thesis

The business should evolve through three transformations:

```text
PHASE 1
"I clean houses."

        ↓

PHASE 2
"I run a cleaning company."

        ↓

PHASE 3
"I operate a system that delivers
reliable home cleaning at scale."
```

## Most important principles

1. **Sell reliability, not cleaning.**
2. **Make pricing understandable before the customer contacts you.**
3. **Turn one-time customers into recurring customers.**
4. **Use Rexburg's student turnover as a seasonal acquisition engine.**
5. **Use landlords/property managers as recurring B2B customers.**
6. **Document cleaning quality with checklists and photos.**
7. **Standardize before hiring aggressively.**
8. **Build technology only after the manual process works.**
9. **Track profit per labor hour, not just revenue.**
10. **Expand geographically only after the operating model is repeatable.**

## North-star customer promise

> **Book it. We show up. It's cleaned exactly as promised. You don't have to think about it again.**
